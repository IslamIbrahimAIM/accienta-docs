# Change requests

Need something adjusted, added, or clarified? There are two easy ways to reach
the delivery team, and both create a tracked request, so nothing gets lost in
email.

## Suggest an improvement

Every page has a **Suggest an Improvement** button at the bottom. It opens a
pre-filled request tied to the exact page you are on, with fields for:

- The section you are referring to
- Your feedback
- The change you would like

Submit it and the Growth Factors team picks it up directly.

## What makes a good change request

To turn a request around quickly, include:

1. **Where** - the capability or page it relates to.
2. **What** - the current behaviour and what you would like instead.
3. **Why** - the business reason, so we design the right solution.

## What happens next

Each request is reviewed, scoped, and scheduled. Anything that changes a live
capability is tested before release and then reflected on the
[Home dashboard](index.md) under Latest updates.
