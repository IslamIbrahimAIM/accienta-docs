# CRM

Manage the full customer relationship, from first contact to won business.

## What it delivers

- **Lead management** - capture and qualify inbound and outbound leads in one place.
- **Opportunity pipeline** - track deals through clear stages with expected value and close dates.
- **Customer tracking** - a single record per customer, aware of their subscriptions and history.
- **Activity follow-up** - scheduled next actions so nothing slips.

## How your team uses it

Sales staff work opportunities through the pipeline; managers see the pipeline
value and conversion at a glance. Customer records connect directly to
quotations and invoices, so the commercial history is always one click away.

Current delivery status and completion are shown on the
[Home dashboard](../index.md) and in [Delivered Modules](index.md).
