# Tax & Localization (UAE & KSA)

Stay compliant in both markets, with the right tax treatment and e-invoicing
built in.

## What it delivers

- **UAE setup** - the UAE company configured on the official localization with
  the shared chart of accounts and correct VAT handling.
- **Saudi setup** - the Saudi company configured for local requirements,
  including ZATCA e-invoicing.
- **Consistent accounts** - both companies share the same account structure for
  clean group reporting.

## How your team uses it

Invoices raised in either company apply the correct tax and compliance rules
automatically. Finance does not maintain two separate rulebooks; the platform
handles the regional differences.

Current delivery status and completion are shown on the
[Home dashboard](../index.md) and in [Delivered Modules](index.md).
