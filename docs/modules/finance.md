# Accounting & Finance

A finance setup tuned to your business, with clean documents your customers
trust.

## What it delivers

- **Shared chart of accounts** - a consistent account structure across the
  group.
- **Customer invoicing** - branded invoices with the correct bank and payment
  details presented clearly.
- **Bank presentation** - bank accounts shown by name and currency where it
  matters, so the right account is always chosen.
- **Partner setup** - customers and vendors classified cleanly for accurate
  reporting.
- **Document layout** - portal and printed documents refined for a
  professional client experience.

## How your team uses it

Finance issues invoices that carry the right branding, bank details, and
payment references. Reporting reflects a clean account and partner structure,
so cost and revenue read correctly per area.

Current delivery status and completion are shown on the
[Home dashboard](../index.md) and in [Delivered Modules](index.md).
