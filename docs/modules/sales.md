# Sales & Quoting

Turn opportunities into accurate, profitable quotations built on your real
channel economics.

## What it delivers

- **Quotations** - professional, branded quotes generated in a few clicks.
- **Channel pricing** - suggested retail price cascades through distributor
  discounts, co-terms, and fees automatically, so pricing is consistent.
- **Margin visibility** - distributor cost and resulting margin are shown on
  the order, line by line and in total.
- **Product catalogue** - a maintained product master with bulk spreadsheet
  import to keep the catalogue current.

## How your team uses it

A salesperson selects products, applies the agreed distributor terms, and the
system computes price and margin in real time. Managers approve on margin, not
guesswork. The finished quotation flows straight into invoicing and
procurement.

Current delivery status and completion are shown on the
[Home dashboard](../index.md) and in [Delivered Modules](index.md).
