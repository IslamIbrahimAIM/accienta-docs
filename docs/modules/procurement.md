# Procurement Automation

Remove manual purchase-order work by generating vendor orders straight from
confirmed sales.

## What it delivers

- **Automatic vendor purchase orders** - when a customer invoice is posted, the
  matching vendor purchase order is created and confirmed, with the correct
  distributor cost already applied.
- **Default vendor selection** - choose the fulfilling vendor per order, from
  your configured default vendors or the full vendor list.
- **Clean procurement flow** - the purchase document is streamlined to the
  states your team actually uses.

## How your team uses it

Sales confirms and invoices as normal; procurement is generated behind the
scenes with no re-keying. Buyers see confirmed orders ready to place with the
right vendor and cost.

Current delivery status and completion are shown on the
[Home dashboard](../index.md) and in [Delivered Modules](index.md).
