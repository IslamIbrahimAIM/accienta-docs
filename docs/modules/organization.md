# Organization & Access

A clear company structure that drives who can see and do what.

## What it delivers

- **Organizational structure** - departments and roles defined as the single
  source of truth for the company.
- **Access control** - permissions aligned to roles, so people reach exactly
  what their job needs.
- **Foundation for growth** - future capabilities build on this structure
  rather than reinventing it.

## How your team uses it

New users are set up against defined roles instead of ad-hoc permissions.
Access stays consistent as the team grows, and reporting can follow the real
organizational lines.
